Priority Model

[[Explain that stronger signals suppress weaker follow-ups so only one action owns the account.]]

Priority Engagement State Purpose
1 Event Lead Lead [[Initial contact from an event or imported lead list.]]
2 Registered Potential [[Contact created an account or profile.]]
3 No Order Potential [[Registered contact reached the no-order threshold.]]
4 Sample Request Potential [[Contact requested a sample, trial, or evaluation path.]]
5 Incomplete Order Potential [[Contact started but did not complete an order.]]
6 First Order Converted [[Conversion event; closes the pre-customer workflow.]]
